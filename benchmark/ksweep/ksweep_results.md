# k-sweep: efficiency vs scoring-accuracy trade-off (full & simplify, k = 3..10)

> This is **Figure 4** in the manuscript (panels a: mean expansions, b: solved rate,
> c: Spearman vs full@50), generated by `../../paper_chem_syn/figures/make_fig4_ksweep.py`.

**Gold standard** = full (unconstrained) model at expansion width **k = 50**,
per-molecule bb-coverage. **Sweep** = both the full and the simplify model at
expansion width **k = 3..10**, everything else fixed (time limit 8 s, <=100 node
expansions, depth 5, 60 s hard cap). All on the **1000 ChEMBL targets**. Per-molecule
score / expansions / wall-clock are recorded in `gold_full_k50.csv`,
`simplify_k03..k10.csv`, `full_k03..k10.csv` (kept for deeper analysis).

Drivers: `../target_set/run_ksweep_chembl.sh` (gold + simplify), `run_ksweep_full.sh`
(full). Analysis: `python ksweep_analyze.py`.

Gold (full@k=50): mean expansions **38.0**, median time **1.21 s**, solved **83.9%**.

Accuracy is measured on the **SynScore** = 1/(U+1)^U (U = non-purchasable starting
materials), the current SynOmega score, vs the full@50 gold.

## Observations

- **Simplify is cheaper than full at every k** (both expansions and wall-clock):
  e.g. k=10 simplify 32.0 exp / 0.31 s vs full 38.8 exp / 0.50 s. Simplify@10
  (0.31 s) is **~4x faster** than the full@50 gold (1.21 s).
- **Simplify reaches a higher solve rate**: simplify@10 solves 85.1%, above both
  full@10 (81.8%) and the full@50 gold (83.9%).
- **Score fidelity to full@50 differs by model.** The full model converges toward
  its own k=50 gold as k grows (Spearman 0.66 -> 0.85) -- same model, more
  candidates. The simplify model, being a *different* model, tracks the full@50
  SynScore less tightly (Spearman 0.53 -> 0.61), yet agrees on the binary solved
  outcome 90% of the time at k=10.
- **Diminishing returns set in around k = 6-8** for both models (accuracy and solve
  rate flatten), so the paper's operating point k=10 is comfortably near-converged.

Raw per-molecule scores and timings are in the 17 CSVs for any deeper analysis
(e.g. where simplify and full@50 disagree, per-bin error, cost-vs-accuracy fronts).
